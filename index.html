About Evan

Evan Yuan is a dancer and choreographer based in Cupertino, California. He has trained at Dance Academy USA since 2013 in contemporary, tap, jazz, hip hop, and ballet, and studies dance composition and choreography at Harker Upper School.

A Hollywood VIBE Senior Company member since 2024, Evan assists convention classes and trains and performs on regional tours and at Nationals. His honors include a Top 5 Dancer of the Year finish at Hollywood VIBE Nationals and ICON of the Year regional titles at Hollywood Connection.

Evan’s choreography spans solos, ensembles, and dance film, including Creep, Mechanical Mind, and Hotel California. He is interested in the connections between movement and cognition, bringing that curiosity to his creative work and interdisciplinary study.

Through FCSN Special Melodies, he also helps lead choir and dance programming for children and young adults with special needs.
