Evan Yuan

Dancer | Choreographer | Performer

About Me

Evan is a dynamic and expressive dancer specializing in contemporary and modern dance. With a passion for storytelling through movement, he has performed in various productions across the country, captivating audiences with his powerful and emotional performances.

Dance Résumé

Nationally recognized dancer with intensive training across a broad range of dance styles and experience choreographing solo and ensemble works. Interested in exploring the connections between movement and cognition through interdisciplinary study.
